# ai-notes

Personal AI learning log, concept notes, paper summaries, and runnable experiments (code & notebooks).

## Structure

- `log/` — Chronological study history (`YYYY/YYYY-MM.md`). Quick daily/weekly entries linking out to notes, papers, and experiments.
- `topics/` — Evergreen concept notes organized by subject (created on demand).
- `papers/` — Paper summaries (`YYYY-short-title.md`).
- `experiments/` — Self-contained code, notebooks, and reproductions (`YYYY-MM-slug/`).
- `courses/` — Notes and exercises tied to a specific course or book.

## Workflow

1. **Record as you go** in `log/YYYY/YYYY-MM.md` with links to whatever you worked on.
2. **Write reusable notes** in `topics/` or `papers/` instead of burying long explanations inside the log.
3. **Keep experiments self-contained** in `experiments/YYYY-MM-slug/` with a short `README.md` and their own dependency file (`pyproject.toml` or `requirements.txt`).
4. **Keep the repo light** — weights, datasets, and caches are gitignored; note download links or reproduction commands instead.
